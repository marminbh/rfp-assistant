# Marmin UAE E-Invoicing — Product Overview

Marmin AI provides an enterprise UAE e-invoicing platform for FTA-mandated electronic invoicing, Peppol document exchange, multi-entity tax operations, and ERP-connected invoice lifecycle management.

## Positioning

- Delivered primarily as a managed SaaS platform on cloud IaaS (Oracle Cloud Infrastructure for UAE production designs described in sources).
- Also offered as dedicated/single-tenant cloud, hybrid (core on-prem + Marmin ASP/Access Point), and fully on-premise models.
- Aligned with UAE e-invoicing / 5-corner framework and Peppol network exchange.
- Designed for multi-entity structures with centralized visibility (Operational, Compliance, and Strategic MIS layers referenced for enterprise proposals).

## Out-of-the-box capabilities (SaaS)

- Electronic invoice generation and management
- Invoice validation
- Submission to regulatory authorities (FTA)
- Invoice exchange with trading partners (Peppol)
- API integrations
- Reporting
- Role-based access control
- Audit logging
- Secure document storage
