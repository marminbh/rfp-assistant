# Marmin Oman (OMN) E-Invoicing — Product Overview

Marmin provides an Oman e-invoicing platform for electronic invoice creation, validation, Peppol document exchange, multi-entity operations, and ERP-connected invoice lifecycle management.

## Positioning

- Delivered primarily as a managed SaaS platform, with dedicated/single-tenant and hybrid/on-prem options available for enterprise needs.
- Aligned with Oman e-invoicing workflows and Peppol network exchange.
- Supports sale and purchase document flows, party/profile management, and partner operations.

## Out-of-the-box capabilities

- Electronic invoice generation and management
- Invoice validation
- Peppol transmission and status tracking
- Purchase and sales document APIs
- API integrations and webhooks
- Reporting and audit logging
- Role-based access control
- Secure document storage (PDF/XML artifacts)

## Go-to-market notes

- Local and regional Marmin teams support Oman implementations.
- Product documentation for APIs and document types is maintained in the Oman docs portal.
