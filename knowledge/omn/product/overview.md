# Marmin Oman (OMN) E-Invoicing — Product Overview

Marmin provides an Oman e-invoicing platform for electronic invoice creation, validation, Peppol document exchange, multi-entity operations, and ERP-connected invoice lifecycle management.

## Positioning

- Delivered as **SaaS**, **PaaS**, or **On-Prem** (also cloud-ready / cloud-agnostic; IaaS hosting supported).
- Aligned with Oman e-invoicing workflows, Peppol network exchange, **PINT Oman (PINT OM)**, and Oman Tax Authority (OTA) requirements.
- Supports B2B, B2G, and B2C issuance; invoice receipt; tax reporting to OTA; party/profile management; and partner operations.
- Integrates with core banking, XVAT, financial/AP systems, ERP/procurement, and enterprise ESB / API gateways.

## Oman tax authority alignment

- Electronic invoicing using the **5-Corner Model** for secure, standardized invoice exchange between businesses, service providers, and the **Oman Tax Authority (OTA)**.
- Upgrade / align existing **XVAT** (VAT) systems to OTA requirements based on the OTA **Data Dictionary**.
- Issue VAT invoices electronically in the OTA-approved format via an **OTA-approved ASP** (Authenticated Service Provider).
- E-invoicing integrated with XVAT (and FA / input-side systems for import-of-services where required) to issue **validated tax invoices**.
- Enrich invoice data to **PINT OM** specifications (hardcoded and logically derived fields supported).
- Report AR and AP transactions to the **OTA Access Point** via Peppol; maintain acknowledgements and submission history.

## Out-of-the-box capabilities

- Taxpayer / Peppol participant onboarding (SML/SMP registration and status)
- B2B/B2G and B2C invoice and credit-note issuance
- B2C QR code generation and PDF invoices with QR
- Invoice receipt from Peppol and routing to ERP/procurement
- Invoice validation (business rules + Peppol BIS / XML schema)
- Peppol transmission, delivery acknowledgements, and lifecycle status
- Purchase and sales document APIs; webhooks
- Dashboards and reports (Excel, CSV, PDF export)
- Error classification, notifications, configurable auto-retry, and manual reprocess
- Reporting and audit logging; RBAC; maker-checker
- Secure document storage (PDF/XML artifacts)
- Arabic language interface; configurable customer logo
