# Oman E-Invoicing Compliance

## Scope

Marmin Oman e-invoicing supports electronic invoice exchange using Peppol-oriented flows and Oman-relevant document/party data (including Oman subdivision and address-scheme codelists).

## Solution alignment

- Structured electronic invoice payloads suitable for Oman Peppol exchange
- Party identification and validation required for submission/receipt
- Peppol status tracking and status logs for operational follow-up
- Retention of document artifacts (XML/PDF) for audit support
